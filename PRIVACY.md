# Privacy policy — Loop Workspace to Markdown

Last updated: 2026-11-20

**What the extension reads.** When you click Export, it reads the page list from the Microsoft Loop workspace open in your browser and the content of those pages (`https://loop.cloud.microsoft/*`). It reads nothing on any other website.

**Where that data goes.** Nowhere outside your computer. The extension has no servers, no analytics and no tracking, and makes no network requests of its own. Page content is turned into Markdown inside your browser and saved to your Downloads folder.

**What is stored.** The copied pages of your most recent workspace export, and of your most recent single-page export, are kept in the extension's local browser storage. That lets an interrupted export continue and a finished export be downloaded again. Starting a new export replaces them; uninstalling the extension deletes them.

**Sharing.** No data is sold or shared with anyone.

**Contact.** Open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
